A structure for difficult conversations

You both want to work it out. Somehow the conversation still turns into an argument.

WIN WIN is a game that gives two people a structure for having a difficult conversation — step by step, without trying to decide who is right.

When it feels familiar

For the moments when the relationship matters, but talking feels difficult.

With a partner, friend, parent, adult child, sibling, or another person close to you.

What is often missing

When a conversation is already tense, another piece of advice is rarely what you need.

Sometimes what helps is a way to slow down, let each person finish, understand what sits behind each position, and move gradually toward a possible next step.

What can change

The goal is not to decide who was right.

WIN WIN cannot guarantee that a conflict disappears. It can help you understand what the conflict is actually about and find a possible next step together.

It is not therapy and it is not a way to avoid difficult conversations. It is a structure for having one differently.
Usually
“You never listen to me.”
After slowing it down
“When this happens, I feel like what I say does not matter.”
